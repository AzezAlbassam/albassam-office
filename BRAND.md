# Albassam · Falaj

One spring. Every share.

## The one-sentence brief

A private family office that shows the family's money the way an old Arabian
falaj shows water: one spring, shared by channels, reaching each person by their
share, calm, and always accounted for.

## Why the falaj

A falaj divides one spring's water among families by fixed shares. Aziz's pool
works the same way: one pool, members own units, and whatever the pool pays flows to
each person by their units. The metaphor is the model, so the design teaches the
accounting without a word of jargon.

## Color

Deep water `#12312E` is the ground, sampled from the hero film's final frame.
Limestone `#EDE6D8` is the text. Spring water `#7FD6C4` is the single accent, used
only where money moves or the user acts. Clay `#E59A7E` means a loss or a deduction,
nothing else. Gains are `#93D3A8`. Never a pure black or white canvas.

## Typography

Newsreader carries every heading and every money figure; Hanken Grotesk carries
everything read and every control; Martian Mono appears only in tiny labels.

## The share rule

Shares follow the money put in: money in buys shares at $1.00 each, money out
cancels shares at today's price. The page always shows everyone's new share before
anything is saved.

## Imagery

One world: the falaj from directly above in soft morning light. The hero film
descends from the channel into the basin. Generated on Higgsfield.

## Voice

Calm, safe, plain, honest. Short sentences in sentence case. No exclamation points, no
em dashes, no finance jargon a family member would not follow. English only.
Keep it simple: no extra ratios or calculators unless Aziz asks.

## Privacy

The office is private. Nothing is read before the owner's Google sign-in, and the
Firestore rules enforce it for every document under `familyOffice/`.
